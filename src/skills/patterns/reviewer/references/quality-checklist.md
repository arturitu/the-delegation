# Quality Checklist: General Artifacts

## 1. Compliance (Critical)
- [ ] Does it meet all the explicitly mentioned requirements in the brief?
- [ ] Is the format correct (JSON, Markdown, etc.)?

## 2. Technical Quality (Major)
- [ ] Is the logic sound?
- [ ] Are there any obvious errors or anti-patterns?
- [ ] Is the code/text readable and well-structured?

## 3. Style (Minor)
- [ ] Does it follow the Tone & Voice guidelines?
- [ ] Are there typos or grammatical errors?
